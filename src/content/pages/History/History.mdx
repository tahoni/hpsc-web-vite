The founding of the {clubName} was a dream come true for a group of
young and energetic shooting enthusiasts from the Hartbeespoort area. Prior to
the forming of the club, members were mainly shooting under the colours of
both Premier and Akasia Shooting Clubs.

Most of the founder members stay in Hartbeespoort, an active community which
had been without a practical pistol club for many years. Premier Club was a
day’s travel by horse, and Akasia closed its doors.

The club was founded in 2003, without a designated shooting range. The range
at NECSA, Pelindaba was used in the beginning, but it was soon realised that
this range was unsuitable for practical pistol shooting. Further negotiations
resulted in an agreement whereby the Hartbeespoortdam club could use the
{clubShootingRangeDescription} Range at Eeufees road, and this range is still used
both for practice and competitions.

It will always be a dream of most members to have a shooting range in Hartbeespoort…

We compete under <a href={internationalAssociationWebsite} target="_blank">{internationalAssociationName} ({internationalAssociationAbbreviation})</a>
rules, and encourage members to participate in three gun competitions.
Shooting is scheduled as per a pre-arranged calendar that can be found on this
website.

and the
<a href={provincialAssociationWebsite} target="_blank">{provincialAssociationName} ({provincialAssociationAbbreviation})</a>
